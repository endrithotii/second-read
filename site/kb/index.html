Resistance to osimertinib in EGFR-mutant lung cancer: evidence entries, measured against expert curators

Papers become structured evidence entries (gene, variant, drug, disease, evidence type, direction, significance, level, and the exact sentence or table cell they rest on), each compared with the knowledge base. Every number below is measured against CIViC's curators on papers held out from prompt development, with a 95% interval.

Generated 2026-10-28 21:11 UTC. Ground truth: 385 CIViC entries from 155 papers (111 papers held out, 44 used to build the prompts).

Read this first

  • The narrow topic is too small to measure on its own. CIViC holds 13 curated entries on resistance to osimertinib. The evaluation therefore uses all CIViC evidence on EGFR inhibitors in lung cancer, and reports the osimertinib-resistance subset separately, with its wide interval.
  • CIViC is not complete. An entry CIViC lacks is not automatically wrong, so every precision figure measured against CIViC is a floor. The blind expert review below is what measures it properly.
  • Most papers have no open full text. 77 of 111 evaluated papers could only be read as abstracts; results are split by source.
  • Progress: 111 of 111 held-out papers extracted, 300 of 302 screening papers screened. Free model quotas limit how much runs per day; the numbers update as runs complete.

8. Blind expert review

The reviewer does not see the models' reasoning or the routing. Error categories are fixed in advance: correct; wrong variant or molecular profile; wrong drug; wrong disease; wrong significance or direction; wrong evidence level; the quote does not support the entry; out of scope (not EGFR-TKI evidence in lung cancer); duplicate of another entry.
